# CAN Sim Tool

## Register

product

## Users

CAN engineers configuring frames, inspecting traffic and testing trigger responses on Windows.

## Product Purpose

Make CAN configuration, live inspection and offline analysis precise and predictable.

## Brand Personality

Simple, professional, practical. Confirmed with the user on 2026-09-14.

## Anti-references

Decorative dashboards, oversized cards, animations that distract from changing data.

## Design Principles

- Preserve data, focus and unsaved edits during presentation changes.
- Prioritize readable byte editors and stable receive views.
- Keep navigation and operation groups consistent.
- Support Chinese and English without changing protocol values or user content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, responsive layouts and persistent language/theme preferences.
